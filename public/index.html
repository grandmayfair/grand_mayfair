A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good holidays start
with clear answers.

How does holiday membership work?+

Start with your preferred destinations, holiday time and travelling companions. The team will explain the available plan options, entitlements, fees and terms so you can decide with a clear picture.

Can I plan a holiday in India or overseas?+

Yes. Share the places on your wishlist, whether a heritage city in India, a mountain retreat or an international escape. The team will discuss suitable options and current availability.

Does an enquiry confirm a booking?+

An enquiry starts a conversation with the travel team. A reservation is confirmed separately after you review availability, prices, inclusions and the applicable terms.

Can the itinerary be shaped around my family?+

Tell us who is travelling, your preferred pace and any special requests. These details help the team discuss an itinerary and stay options that suit your group.
