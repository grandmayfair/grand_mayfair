A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good holidays start
with clear answers.

How can I explore membership?

Choose “Discover your membership” and create a brief with your travel interests. Ask your advisor for the current plan options, eligibility, inclusions, fees, and terms before choosing a membership.

Can I plan a holiday in India or overseas?

Use the destination collection for inspiration, or add a different destination in the planner. Your advisor can confirm suitable stay options, travel requirements, and availability for your dates.

Does an enquiry confirm a booking?

Your enquiry is received by the travel team. Your advisor will discuss availability, pricing, and booking terms. Reservations are confirmed separately.

Can I tailor my trip?

Yes. Add your preferred destination, dates, travellers, and travel style to the brief. The notes field gives you room for special requests and places you would like to visit.
