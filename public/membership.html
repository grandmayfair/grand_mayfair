YOUR MEMBERSHIP QUESTIONS

Confidence starts
with clear answers.

How do I choose the right membership?+

Start with how often you want to travel, who will join you and the places you would like to explore. Request the available approved plan details and compare them against your holiday preferences.

What should I review before joining?+

Ask for the plan duration, night entitlement, occupancy, seasons, booking lead times, fees, additional costs, change conditions and cancellation terms in writing.

Is a particular stay or date guaranteed?+

Availability and booking conditions depend on the approved plan and the requested arrangements. Confirm your specific stay, travel dates and inclusions with the team before making plans.

Where can I see my membership records?+

Sign in to My Mayfair with your verified account. Approved membership details, linked travel requests and booking records are available in your customer workspace.
